## What's in v0.5

- **Locks are no longer missed.** Opening stories, reels or DMs inside an app used to pause the timer. Now it keeps counting.
- **Locks open reliably on Android 15 and newer.** A dark lock layer covers the app the moment the limit hits and pauses its audio. Then the full lock screen opens.
- **Last 5 minutes.** Every limited app and group shows how much you used it in the last 5 minutes.
- **Killed-guard warning.** If Android stopped the guard in the background, the Today screen tells you when and how to prevent it.

### Also in this build
- Opt-in limits: only the apps you add (or put in a group) are locked
- Per-app limits and cooldowns, plus groups that share one timer (a Social group is pre-filled)
- Optional motivational videos from your gallery on the lock screen
- NFC tags: set up stickers, scan them, and see your scan history (home actions are coming)
