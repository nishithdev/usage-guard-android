# Privacy Policy for Usage Guard

*Last updated: November 12, 2026*

Usage Guard is an Android app that limits how long you stay in the apps you choose. This policy explains what the app reads, where it keeps that information, and how to delete it.

**In short:** everything stays on your phone. Usage Guard has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect, sell or share any of your data.

## What the app reads, and why

| Information | Why it's needed | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| **Which app is open and for how long** (Android's Usage access) | To time the apps you chose to limit, lock them when the limit is reached, and show your usage on the Today screen | On your phone only |
| **The list of apps installed on your phone** | So you can pick which apps to limit | On your phone only |
| **Videos you pick from your gallery** (optional) | To play them on the lock screen | The app keeps a reference to the videos you picked. It doesn't copy or upload them, and it can't see any other photos or videos. |
| **NFC stickers you set up** (optional) | To recognize your stickers and keep a scan history | Tag names, sticker serial numbers and scan times stay on your phone only |
| **Your settings** (limits, groups, cooldowns) | To remember your choices | On your phone only |

## Permissions

- **Usage access.** Lets the app see which app is in front and for how long.
- **Display over other apps.** Lets the app show the cooldown lock on top of a limited app.
- **Notifications** (optional). Shows the "Guard on" notice that keeps tracking running.
- **NFC.** Lets the app read and write the NFC stickers you set up.
- **Run at startup.** Restarts the guard after your phone reboots.

Usage Guard does **not** request internet access, so it has no way to send anything off your phone.

## Sharing

None. No data is collected by the developer or shared with third parties.

## Children

Usage Guard is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

## Deleting your data

All data is stored inside the app on your phone. To delete it, either:
- go to Settings → Apps → Usage Guard → Storage → **Clear storage**, or
- uninstall the app.

Removing a video or a tag inside the app also removes it from the app's storage.
