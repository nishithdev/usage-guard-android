## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Stop losing time to endless scrolling. Usage Guard puts a hard cooldown on the apps you choose.

Pick the apps you lose time in. When you've used one for 2 minutes straight, it locks behind a countdown screen, and it won't open again until the cooldown is over. There's no "5 more minutes" button.

HOW IT WORKS
• You choose what's limited. Only the apps you switch on are ever locked. Everything else is just counted.
• Continuous-use limits. The timer counts while you stay in the app. Step away for 30 seconds and the next visit starts fresh.
• A real cooldown. When the limit hits, a full-screen lock takes over and pauses what was playing. Reopening the app brings the lock straight back until the timer ends.

MADE FOR HOW PEOPLE ACTUALLY SCROLL
• Groups: put Instagram, X and Snapchat in one "Social" group so they share one timer. Jumping between them doesn't reset it, and they all lock together.
• Per-app rules: give YouTube 10 minutes and a 20-minute lock, and keep 2 minutes for everything else.
• Motivational videos: optionally play videos you pick from your gallery on the lock screen.

SEE WHERE YOUR TIME GOES
• Today screen with total screen time and an hourly chart
• Time, sessions and cooldowns for every app
• How long you've used each limited app in the last 5 minutes

NFC TAGS
Set up NFC stickers around your home and tap them with your phone. Choosing what each tag does is coming in a future update.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission: nothing ever leaves your phone
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Gallery videos are chosen with Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the videos you pick

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Usage access: to see which app is open and for how long
• Display over other apps: to show the cooldown lock
• Notifications (optional): the "Guard on" notice that keeps tracking running

Usage Guard is open source: github.com/nishithdev/usage-guard-android
```
